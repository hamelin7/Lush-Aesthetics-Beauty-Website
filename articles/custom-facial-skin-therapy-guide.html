Facials: The Ultimate Guide to Custom Skin Therapy

Category: Facial Aesthetics Read Time: 4 min read Location: Westfield, MA
The Bottom Line

A custom facial goes beyond basic skincare by targeting your exact skin concerns, from acne and congestion to aging and dryness. By combining deep cleansing, professional exfoliation, and targeted serums, regular facials promote cellular turnover and lasting hydration.

Taking care of your skin requires more than just a daily cleanser and moisturizer. While consistency at home is crucial, professional custom facial treatments provide corrective benefits that elevate your skin's health to a whole new level.

Why do I need a professional facial?

At-home skincare is maintenance, but professional facials are corrective. A licensed aesthetician can safely perform extractions, use higher-grade active ingredients, and utilize specialized modalities (like steam, LED, and targeted facial massage) to stimulate blood flow and lymphatic drainage.

During a custom session at Lush Aesthetics & Beauty, your treatment is completely tailored to what your skin needs in that moment—whether that is clearing clogged pores, infusing deep hydration, or restoring a youthful elasticity.

How often should I get a facial?

For optimal skin health, aim for a facial every 4 to 6 weeks. This timeframe aligns with your skin's natural cellular turnover cycle (the rate at which new skin cells travel from the deepest layer to the surface).

Regular monthly facials ensure dead skin cells are cleared away before they clog pores, preventing stubborn breakouts, uneven texture, and dullness while keeping active ingredients absorbing efficiently.

What makes custom skin therapy different?

Unlike generic "one-size-fits-all" spa facials, custom skin therapy evaluates your skin barrier, hydration levels, lifestyle factors, and specific goals. We blend clinical exfoliation with soothing botanicals to achieve visible results without compromise.
