Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Lush Aesthetics & Beauty, we believe in being completely transparent about how we collect and use data related to you. This simple, plain-English policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding our website's tracking.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are downloaded to your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you interact with the site, and improve your overall browsing experience.

How We Use Cookies

Our website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: These are required for the website to function properly, such as remembering whether you have accepted or declined our cookie policy.
  • Analytics Cookies: With your explicit consent, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site (like which pages are most popular and how long users stay). This helps us improve our website and services. No personally identifiable information is collected or tracked.

Your Privacy Choices

We respect your privacy. You have complete control over whether we track your visit:

  • Opt-in Tracking: Our Google Analytics tracking scripts will only load if you explicitly click the "OK" button on our cookie consent banner.
  • Opt-out / Decline: If you decline or ignore the banner, we will not load analytics cookies on your device.
  • Changing Your Mind: You can clear your browser's local storage or cookies at any time to reset your preference and see our banner again.

Booking Information

Please note that when you click "Book Now", you are directed to our secure Square Appointments portal. Square has its own privacy and cookie policies governing the booking process, which you can review on their platform.
